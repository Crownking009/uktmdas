About TMDAS

Food that brings people together

TMDAS Catering Services serves authentic Nigerian food in the UK. Our promise is on every menu: good food, great taste, always fresh.

We cook with traditional recipes and real ingredients, from smoky jollof and rich egusi to peppered turkey and golden puff puff.

Whether you want a quick lunch or a feast for the whole family, we cook it as if it were for our own table.
